

# Phase 7: Do We Need a PEO Page

Build out the "Do We Need a PEO?" page for first-time buyers evaluating whether a PEO is the right fit.

## Sections

### 1. Hero
- Navy background, headline: "Not sure if a PEO is the right move?"
- Subhead about it being a common question with no one-size-fits-all answer
- Dual CTAs: "Compare My Options" + "See How It Works"

### 2. Signs You Might Be Ready
- 6 cards: growing past 10 employees, spending too much time on HR, struggling with benefits, compliance concerns, multi-state workforce, high turnover
- Headline: "Signs it might be time."

### 3. What a PEO Actually Does
- 4-5 cards demystifying PEO services: co-employment model, benefits access, payroll/tax admin, compliance support, workers comp
- Headline: "What you are actually getting."

### 4. When a PEO Might Not Be the Fit
- Honest section with 3-4 scenarios where a PEO isn't ideal: very small teams (<5), highly specialized benefits needs, organizations that need full HR control
- Builds trust through transparency

### 5. How It Works (condensed)
- 3-step mini-timeline reused from other ICP pages
- Link to /how-it-works

### 6. Final CTA
- "Still not sure? That is what we are here for." with dual CTAs
