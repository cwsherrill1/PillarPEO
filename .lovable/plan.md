

# Phase 5: Switch Your PEO Page

Build out the "Switch Your PEO" ICP page — targeting companies unhappy with their current PEO provider who need to evaluate alternatives.

## Sections

### 1. Hero
- Navy background, headline: "Your PEO is not working. Now what?"
- Subhead about the pain of being stuck with a bad PEO fit
- Dual CTAs: "Compare My Options" + "See How It Works"

### 2. Pain Points
- Headline: "Signs it is time to switch."
- 6 cards covering common frustrations: poor service, rising costs, bad tech, compliance gaps, rigid contracts, lack of transparency

### 3. What We Do Differently
- Headline: "We help you evaluate without starting from scratch."
- 4-5 bullet/card items: side-by-side comparison, contract review, transition planning, onboarding support, ongoing check-ins

### 4. How It Works (condensed)
- 3-step mini-timeline referencing the full process: Tell us what's broken → We compare alternatives → Transition with support
- Link to /how-it-works for full details

### 5. Final CTA
- "Ready to explore better options?" with dual CTAs matching site pattern
