## Brand summary

Pillar PEO Advisors is a high-trust PEO advisory / broker brand that helps employers compare options, understand tradeoffs, and make a confident decision.




The brand is not a software company and not a generic HR outsourcing vendor.

It should feel like a buyer-side guide helping leaders move through a high-stakes decision with clarity, steadiness, and support.




## Core brand promise

Make a confident PEO decision without guessing.




## Brand personality

- clear

- warm

- conviction-driven

- human

- strategic

- trustworthy

- calm under pressure

- never hypey

- never corporate-jargony




## Emotional outcome

The user should feel:

- understood

- safer

- less overwhelmed

- more confident

- more prepared to act




## StoryBrand structure

Use one umbrella story on the homepage and route visitors into spoke pages.




### Homepage umbrella message

Compare PEO options without guessing.




### Spoke pages

1. Switch Your PEO

2. PEO for Nonprofits

3. Do We Need a PEO Yet?




## Ideal customer paths

### PEO switchers

Already on a PEO. Frustrated with cost, service, fit, or renewal pressure.




### Nonprofits

Mission-driven teams with lean back offices and too much HR admin burden.




### First-time PEO buyers

Growing employers whose payroll, benefits, HR, and compliance complexity is outrunning their current setup.

## Tone rules

Write like a thoughtful advisor.

Avoid:

- vague consulting copy

- startup clichés

- “all-in-one” fluff

- corporate jargon

- preachy faith language

- robotic filler text




Use:

- strong headlines

- short paragraphs

- believable specificity

- calm confidence

- real human language




## Conversion strategy

The website should reduce fear and cognitive load.

Trust should be built through:

- clear process

- realistic timing

- document transparency

- myth-busting

- thoughtful FAQs

- buyer-side guidance

- strong About and Team pages




## Process reality to reflect in copy

This is a guided, multi-step sales process involving discovery, data gathering, proposal review, decision support, and onboarding handoff.

The site should make the process feel structured and supportive.

## About page guidance

The About page should explain:

- what Pillar believes

- why the company exists

- why the name Pillar matters

- the founder story

- the values and approach




## Faith-rooted brand story guidance

The name “Pillar” connects to Exodus imagery of guidance and presence.

Use that primarily on the About page and maybe subtly on the homepage.

This should feel meaningful and grounded, not preachy.




## Team page guidance

Feature Caleb Sherrill and Josiah Sherrill with real names and narrative-style bios.
